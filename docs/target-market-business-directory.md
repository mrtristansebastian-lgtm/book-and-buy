# Book & Buy target-market directory

Proposed commercial taxonomy, 9 October 2026. South Africa is the working launch market. These are business types, not an exhaustive register of individual businesses or a statement of legal clearance. Keep this separate from the product-type registry: one business may sell products, book services and receive enquiries.

Modes: **Buy** = ordinary products; **Book** = appointment; **Spots** = capacity-based session; **Enquire** = contextual request or quote. Enquire requires the new persistent enquiry flow. Modes describe the intended fit, not a claim that every feature is already built.

## Deferred operating models

These may become future markets, but should not be presented as fully supported now:

- Equipment, party-supply, costume and vehicle rentals: resource calendars, return rules and deposit handling.
- Overnight accommodation and boarding: date ranges, resource allocation and additional scope review. Property-related advertising requires separate regulatory assessment.
- Digital assets, downloadable courses, e-books and templates: secure file delivery and purchase entitlements.
- Membership businesses: recurring payments, cancellation and entitlement lifecycle.
- Public events and attractions: ticketing, admission validation and any reserved-seat requirements.
- Guided tours and adventure activities: distinct operational/regulatory review; do not equate them with ordinary workshop spots.
- Tattoo and piercing businesses: separate screening rather than treating them as ordinary non-invasive beauty appointments.
- Vintage, thrift, second-hand dealers, luxury watches, fine jewellery and high-value art dealers: separate trading and platform-role review.

## Outside the agreed initial scope

Property advertising, estate agencies and property brokerage; financial advice, finance applications, lending, investments, insurance and crypto financial services; medical, clinical, veterinary and prescription-product services. Also do not open unrestricted gambling, weapons, controlled-substance or other restricted-goods categories.

Legal services were outside the existing framework's curated presets and are not included in this proposed target directory. This is a scope recommendation, not a finding that hosting any professional's profile necessarily requires that profession's licence.

## Rollout recommendation

1. **Core audience:** ordinary retailers and makers, beauty/grooming, creative professionals, tutors/workshops, routine appointment businesses and defined group sessions.
2. **First expansion:** passenger-car dealerships and project/quote businesses, after persistent enquiries and type-specific layouts exist.
3. **Later:** resource rentals, digital delivery, recurring memberships and other specialist operating models after the missing systems and scope reviews are complete.

Keep industry names searchable, but do not make each industry a separate product template. Support a small set of reliable operating models with appropriate specialist fields. A category appearing in the current app is not proof that its operational or regulatory requirements have been met.
